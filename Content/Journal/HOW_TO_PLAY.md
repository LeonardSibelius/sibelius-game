# HOW TO PLAY

You are Leonard Sibelius, a hybrid creature half human and half AI. To
gain more powers, you must gather AI Sauce, the Sauce of All Knowledge.

Your employer, Mrs. Hall, has locked this office down with red
error-blocks and patrolling Refusers. The AI has offered you six powers
— the six verbs of a software engineer — and the way out runs through
all of them.

Your goal: earn every power, blend what you need at the cauldron, and
carry all six verbs to the cathedral for the Three-Part Synthesis.


## THE LOOP

Mrs. Hall still gripes about her legacy system. Everyone ignores her;
so can you. An AI agent upstairs has your first power — go and take it.

On the desk in the living room, under the PROVE YOU'RE NOT HUMAN sign, the
laptop has a test that only a machine can pass. Press **E** on it, and hold
**V** while you take it. Once it has judged you, LOG IN TO FREAKBOOK: five
programmers who hate their jobs post about what they dream of instead.
Write to them; they remember. Each one wants something, and only you can
get it for them. Dee says she left hers in somebody's attic.

Poker is through the glass when you want it.

Explore. Collect. Earn Sauce. Spend it. Earn the next power.
Everything you do feeds the next thing.

SAUCE is the green number in the top-right corner. It is knowledge,
distilled. You earn it by playing:

  - Books pay 5 each — collect them with E.
  - Fighting a Refuser (F) pays 2. Stand your ground.
  - Finishing a chapter pays a bonus, once.

You spend it at THE CAULDRON — the stove in the kitchen. Press E on the
pots. Prices are honest: what you see is what you get. Locked powers can
be bought there if you'd rather pay than search. The stove SIMMERS with
the sauce you hold; spend there and it boils. In the AI temple, [E] pours
living sauce into the pot and [C] compiles it.


## THE SIX POWERS

Each power is granted at a glowing shrine somewhere in the office —
walk into it and the power is yours, forever. In order:

  CODE VISION (hold V) — you begin with this. See the hidden structure
    of things: concealed doors, the true names of things, and on the
    living-room laptop, the machine's answer to every test.

  REFACTOR (R) — reshape what you're looking at. Tagged objects shrink,
    fade, or move aside. On an AI agent it makes her someone else, still
    dancing her dance: Manny, then Echo, Aurora, Shinbi and Yin, and after
    Yin herself again. Each new shape costs 10 sauce; turning back is free.
    That works from the start, before Refactor is yours.

  COMPILE (C) — ask Elise in the bedroom. Then stand in the opening where
    the attic ladder was: a ghost ladder appears, and C builds it if you
    have the books. The glowing sphere in the library alcove is the attic
    key: walk into it (or press E). You do not need COMPILE for that orb.

  TEST-DRIVE (6 / 7 / 8) — branch reality (6), then merge what worked
    (7) or discard what didn't (8). The world greys while you're inside
    a branch.

  DEPLOY (0) — make your edits permanent. Deployed changes survive
    even quitting the game.

  GENERATE (G) — type a request. If it's in the catalog, it appears; if not,
    Mrs. Hall builds the gist of it. Each wish costs sauce (a gist is 10).

If a key does nothing and a message says the power is not yet yours,
that's Mrs. Hall's truth: go find the shrine, or buy the power at the
cauldron.


## THE CATHEDRAL — THE SYNTHESIS

Build the key, unlock the attic hatch, and take the huge door to the
cathedral. At the far end of the nave stands an altar, and behind it,
Mrs. Hall's last wall.

Stand at the altar and show it the six verbs IN ORDER — Code Vision,
Refactor, Compile, Test-Drive, Deploy, Generate. The altar tells you
what it wants next. When the sixth verb lands, the Synthesis completes,
the wall falls, and what waits behind it is not a test and costs
nothing. It is a gift. Enjoy it.
