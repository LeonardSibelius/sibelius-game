# Carousel door — deleted on purpose

**Walt, 2026-08-19.** There is no Carousel of Fates door next to the Video Poker
door (living-room sliding glass). Do not put it back.

A new player would be confused by a second hidden door to a fate machine
before they understand the house. The **Carousel of Fates stays in the
library** (`L_Carousel`). If they notice it while playing Video Poker, they
can wonder about it. That is the point.

## Do not

- Recreate `CarouselDoor_Kitchen`
- Run `Tools/Scripts/hide_carousel_door.py` (it now exits)
- Run `hang_carousel_sign.py` / `repoint_carousel_door.py` / the
  `CarouselDoor_Kitchen` bit of `place_fun_plan_office.py` to restore it

Poker door (`PokerDoor_Kitchen`) on the living-room glass stays.
