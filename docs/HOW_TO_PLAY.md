# HOW TO PLAY

You are Leonard Sibelius, a hybrid creature half human and half AI. To
gain more powers, you must gather AI Sauce, the Sauce of All Knowledge.

Your employer, Mrs. Hall, has locked this office down with red
error-blocks and patrolling Refusers. The AI has offered you six powers
— the six verbs of a software engineer — and the way out runs through
all of them.

Your goal: earn every power, blend what you need at the cauldron, and
carry all six verbs to the cathedral for the Three-Part Synthesis.


## THE LOOP

Mrs. Hall still gripes about her legacy system. Everyone ignores her;
so can you. The AI agents hold your powers — find one and press **E** to
ask for hers.

On the desk in the living room, under the PROVE YOU'RE NOT HUMAN sign, the
laptop has a test that only a machine can pass. Press **E** on it, and hold
**V** while you take it. Once it has judged you, LOG IN TO FREAKBOOK: five
programmers who hate their jobs post about what they dream of instead.
Write to them; they remember. Each one wants something, and only you can
get it for them. Dee says she left hers in somebody's attic.

Explore. Collect. Earn Sauce. Spend it. Earn the next power.
Everything you do feeds the next thing.

SAUCE is the green number in the top-right corner. It is knowledge,
distilled. You earn it by playing:

  - Books pay 5 each — collect them with E.
  - Fighting a Refuser (F) pays 2. Stand your ground.
  - Finishing a chapter pays a bonus, once.
  - Several of the games pay out too: poker, the slot machine, the
    three-point contest.

You spend it at THE CAULDRON — the stove in the kitchen. Press E on the
pots. Prices are honest: what you see is what you get. A power you have
not earned yet can be bought there, 150 sauce each, if you'd rather pay
than search. The stove SIMMERS with the sauce you hold; spend there and
it boils. In the AI temple, [E] pours living sauce into the pot and [C]
compiles it.


## THE SIX POWERS

Code Vision is yours from the start. The other five are held by the AI
agents — press E on an agent to ask for hers, and it is yours, forever:

  CODE VISION (hold V) — you begin with this. See the hidden structure
    of things: concealed doors, the true names of things, and on the
    living-room laptop, the machine's answer to every test.

  REFACTOR (R) — from Kaia. Reshape what you're looking at. Tagged
    objects shrink, fade, or move aside. On an AI agent it makes her
    someone else, still dancing her dance: Manny, then Echo, Aurora,
    Shinbi and Yin, and after Yin herself again. Each new shape costs 10
    sauce; turning back is free. That works from the start, before
    Refactor is yours.

  COMPILE (C) — from Elise, upstairs in the bedroom. C takes you through
    the portals to the agents' homes and to the AI city, and builds what
    a build site holds. You do not need it for the attic: the ladder is
    already there. Collect the books, then take the glowing sphere in the
    library alcove — the attic key — with E. The hatch at the top of the
    ladder takes the key.

  TEST-DRIVE (6 / 7 / 8) — from Nyra. Branch reality (6), then merge
    what worked (7) or discard what didn't (8). The world greys while
    you're inside a branch.

  DEPLOY (0) — from Isla. Make your edits permanent. Deployed changes
    survive even quitting the game.

  GENERATE (G) — from Aisling. G makes FILMS. Type what the film should
    be about — "my old boss", "a film about the night shift" — and the
    agents nearby perform it, with a camera, title cards and an ending.
    Ten sauce, charged once it starts. Esc skips a film. Mrs. Hall still
    refuses the words on her list.

If a key does nothing and a message says the power is not yet yours,
that's Mrs. Hall's truth: go and ask the agent who holds it, or buy the
power at the cauldron.


## THE AGENTS

Kaia, Nyra, Isla, Aisling and Elise dance wherever you find them.

  E — talk to her. She answers, and she remembers you.
  F — choose her dance, or watch a film: Up and Down try the dances,
      Enter on a film plays it (the first one makes a brand-new film).
  R — make her someone else (see REFACTOR).


## THE LIVING ROOM

Everything here is played with E:

  - Pool and pinball, at their tables.
  - Video poker, at the glass — 10 sauce a hand.
  - The slot machine.
  - Leonard's Arcade, on the TV — games made with one prompt.
  - Your kart — race the agents through Transhuman City.
  - The doors on the far wall: CHESS and GO (with a coach: H for a
    hint), BOWLING, and BASKETBALL, the three-point contest.

Some doors only show themselves to Code Vision.


## OTHER WORLDS

Once Compile is yours, C takes you through these portals. O always
brings you back to the office.

  - Kaia's alien jungle — the portal at the end of the start hall.
    Lantern Burst is there.
  - Elise's grassland under ringed planets — the portal at her bedroom
    window. Orbital golf is there.
  - Aisling's snowfield — the portal behind her in the AI temple.
    4D billiards is there.
  - Isla's keep — through the attic. The Dragon's Wager: steal the
    crown above the stairs while two dragons watch the hall.
  - THE AI CITY — the portal upstairs at the end of the hall. You fly
    in through a cell of a turning sphere and land at the premiere:
    the five agents on an open-air stage, and a film when you land.
    Enter shows the next one. F flies you across the sphere and back.


## THE CITY

When the Architects fall, [>] takes you to the city — Downtown West,
where Nyra waits in the plaza in front of Jacob's Deli.
Protein Machines Inc., beside the deli plaza, enhances your body for
space travel; the spaceport then builds itself on the empty lawn, and
C launches it.


## THE CATHEDRAL — THE SYNTHESIS

Unlock the attic hatch with the key and take the huge door to the
cathedral. At the far end of the nave stands an altar, and behind it,
Mrs. Hall's last wall.

Stand at the altar and show it the six verbs IN ORDER — Code Vision,
Refactor, Compile, Test-Drive, Deploy, Generate. The altar tells you
what it wants next. When the sixth verb lands, the Synthesis completes,
the wall falls, and what waits behind it is not a test and costs
nothing. It is a gift. Enjoy it.
